# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Open Poly a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behavior that keeps this project worth participating in:

- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback — on code, not on people.
- Accepting responsibility, apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and
  learning from them.

Behavior that is not acceptable:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address,
  without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, code
review, and commit messages — and when an individual is officially
representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and will take
appropriate and fair corrective action in response to behavior they deem
inappropriate. They have the right to remove, edit, or reject comments,
commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this
Code of Conduct, and will explain moderation decisions when asked.

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior to the
maintainers of [`eitan27/open-poly`](https://github.com/eitan27/open-poly).
The project has no private mailing address; to reach the maintainers without
posting publicly, use **Security → Report a vulnerability** on the repository,
which opens a private thread visible only to them, and say up front that it's
a Code of Conduct report rather than a security issue. All reports will be
reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy and
security will be respected.

Consequences scale with the behavior: a private warning for a first or minor
incident, a temporary ban from interaction with the project for a sustained or
serious one, and a permanent ban for repeated violations, harassment of an
individual, or aggression toward a group.
